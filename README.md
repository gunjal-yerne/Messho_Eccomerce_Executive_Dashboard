


🛍️ Meesho E-Commerce Sales Executive Dashboard
An interactive Meesho sales dashboard designed to monitor e-commerce performance, explore sales distribution, and evaluate profitability across regions, categories, payment methods, and time.

🎯 Project Objective
The objective of this project is to turn e-commerce sales data into an easy-to-understand visual report. The dashboard provides a high-level view of key performance indicators (KPIs) and lets users explore the data using interactive filters.

📈 Dashboard Visuals
Gross Sales by Category – Compare sales performance across product categories.
Payment Mode Wise Profit – Review profit distribution across COD, Debit Card, Net Banking, UPI, and Wallet.
Region Wise Sales – Understand the contribution of Central, East, North, North-East, South, and West regions.
Monthly Sales Trend – Track sales performance across the months of 2025.
Top 5 Highest Rated States – Compare the highest-rated states shown in the dashboard.
🎛️ Interactive Filters
Use the dashboard slicers to explore the data:
Region: Central, East, North, North-East, South, West
Customer Type: Direct Customer, Reseller
Filters allow users to focus on specific segments and explore how the KPIs and charts change.
🔍 Insights Visible in the Dashboard
North contributes the largest share of regional sales in the displayed breakdown (27%).
East accounts for 23%, followed by South (21%) and West (17%).
North-East contributes the smallest displayed share (3%).
COD has the highest profit total among the payment modes shown.
The monthly sales chart reaches its visible peak around October 2025.
The top-rated states displayed are Jharkhand, Kerala, Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, and West Bengal.
These observations describe the dashboard visualization and may change when filters are applied.

🚀 How to Use
Download or clone this repository.
Open the Excel dashboard file in Microsoft Excel.
Use the Region and Customer Type slicers to filter the dashboard.
Explore the charts and KPI cards to analyze the selected data.
